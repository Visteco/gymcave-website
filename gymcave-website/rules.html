Community rules

Last updated 30 October 2026

GymCave is for people who want to get stronger and help each other do it. Keep it that way.

Not allowed

  • Nudity or sexual content, including in profile pictures, cover photos, posts and exercise videos.
  • Hate, bullying or harassment, including slurs, threats, or mocking someone's body or progress.
  • Offensive names: names, usernames, club names and bios with swearing or abuse.
  • Spam and scams: links, phone numbers, selling or advertising in chat or posts. Use the Shop for adverts.
  • Dangerous advice: promoting steroids, illegal drugs, extreme dieting or anything that could seriously hurt someone.
  • Pretending to be someone else, or using GymCave if you are under 13.

How we keep it safe

  • Photos and videos are checked for nudity before they are posted.
  • Names, captions and chat are filtered for swearing and abuse.
  • Tap ⋯ on any post, message or profile to report it or block the person.
  • Every report is reviewed. We aim to act within 24 hours.

What happens if you break the rules

We can remove content, limit your account or ban it, depending on how serious it is. Serious breaches mean an instant ban. If you think we got it wrong, email support@gymcave.app.
