Questions

Good to know

How much does GymCave cost?

GymCave is free to download and use.

Is it for iPhone or Android?

The iPhone version is in beta testing now. An Android version for Google Play is on the way.

Do I need a gym?

No. Every exercise is tagged for a gym, a home gym or no equipment, and you can filter the list to what you have.

How old do I need to be?

You need to be 13 or older to create an account.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → ⚙︎ → Delete my account. You can also ask us to delete it.
