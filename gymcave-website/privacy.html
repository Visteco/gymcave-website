Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

This policy explains what GymCave collects, why, and what you can do about it. GymCave is run from Rochester, Kent, United Kingdom. If you have any questions, email support@gymcave.app.

What we collect

WhatWhy
Name, surname, email address and passwordTo create and secure your account. Your password is stored encrypted.
Date of birthTo check you are 13 or older and to show your age group in the app. Your date of birth is never shown to other people.
Gender, where you train, level and goal (optional)To suggest workouts that suit you.
Workouts, sets, weights, records and routinesTo run the workout log, progress charts and leaderboards.
Profile and cover photos, post photos and videos, captions and commentsTo show your profile and posts to the people you choose.
Chat messagesTo deliver group chat.
Steps (only if you allow Apple Health or Health Connect)To show your daily steps and the steps leaderboard. We only read steps. We never write to your health data, and we never use it for advertising.
Contacts (only if you tap "Contacts")To find friends who already use GymCave. Contacts are checked on your phone and are not stored by us.
Reports and blocksTo keep the community safe.

Who can see what

Your name, username, profile photo, posts, workouts you share and leaderboard positions can be seen by other GymCave users. Your email address, date of birth and password are never shown to anyone.

Checking photos and chat

To stop nudity and abuse, photos and videos are checked by an automated system before they are posted, and text is checked for offensive words. Content that people report is reviewed by the GymCave team.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data and we don't show you personalised adverts. We use trusted service providers to run GymCave, such as our hosting and database provider (Supabase), and Apple and Google to deliver the app and notifications. They can only use your data to provide their service to us. We may share information if the law requires it.

Where it is stored and for how long

Your data is stored on secure servers, which we aim to keep in the UK or EU. We keep it while you have an account. When you delete your account, your data is deleted within 30 days, except records of serious rule-breaking, which we may keep for up to 12 months.

Our legal reasons (UK GDPR)

We use your data to provide the service you signed up for (contract). We use it to keep the community safe and to improve the app (legitimate interests). Where we ask for permission, like steps and contacts, we rely on your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time in your phone settings.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it or delete it, object to how we use it, or ask us to send it to another service. Email support@gymcave.app. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

GymCave is not for children under 13. If we find out an account belongs to someone under 13, we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top and tell you in the app if the change is important.
