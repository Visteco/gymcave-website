Support

How can we help?

Email us at support@gymcave.app and we'll get back to you, usually within 2 working days.

Found a bug in the beta? Take a screenshot in the app and send it as TestFlight feedback, or email it to us.

Common questions

I forgot my password

On the log-in screen tap "Forgot password" and we'll email you a link to set a new one.

How do I report someone?

Tap ⋯ on the post, message or profile and choose Report. You can also block the person so you never see them again. See the community rules.

My steps aren't showing

Open the iPhone Settings app → Health → Data Access & Devices → GymCave, and turn on Steps.

Can I change the colours or use dark mode?

Yes. Go to Profile and tap 🎨 to choose Auto, Light or Dark, a background colour and an accent colour.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → ⚙︎ → Delete my account. Or see delete your account.
