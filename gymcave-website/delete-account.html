Delete your GymCave account

In the app

  1. Open GymCave and go to your Profile.
  2. Tap ⚙︎ (Account).
  3. Tap Delete my account, then tap again to confirm.

By email

If you can't get into the app, email support@gymcave.app from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my GymCave account".

What gets deleted

Your profile, workouts, photos, videos, posts, comments, reactions, chat messages, follows and leaderboard entries are deleted within 30 days. We may keep a record of reports about rule-breaking and of accounts banned for serious breaches for up to 12 months, to keep the community safe.
